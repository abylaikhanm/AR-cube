Perovskite solar cells are next-generation thin-film photovoltaic devices that use perovskite-structured materials to efficiently absorb light and convert it into electrical energy.

About This Model

This interactive model visualizes the surface structure of a perovskite solar cell material. Surface topology is important because it can influence light absorption, charge transport, and overall photovoltaic performance.

The AR view allows the structure to be inspected in real space, making it easier to understand the geometry and surface features compared with a flat image.
